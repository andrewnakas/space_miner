# 🚀 Space Miner - Cosmic Resource Expedition

An HTML5 vertical space exploration and mining game inspired by Motherload. Build and launch rockets to mine asteroids and planets, upgrade your craft, research technologies, and explore distant galaxies!

## 🎮 How to Play

Simply open `index.html` in any modern web browser to start playing!

## 🕹️ Controls

- **W** - Fire main thruster (move up)
- **A** - Fire left thruster (move left)
- **D** - Fire right thruster (move right)
- **S** - Slow descent
- **SPACE** - Activate mining laser
- **E** - Open shop/upgrades (when at Earth)

## 🎯 Objective

Your mission is to explore space, mine valuable resources from asteroids and planets, and return to Earth to sell your cargo and upgrade your rocket. The farther you venture, the more valuable the resources become!

## ⚠️ Survival Mechanics

### Fuel Management
- **Critical Rule**: You MUST have enough fuel to return to Earth and land safely!
- Running out of fuel in space means your rocket will fall uncontrollably
- Hard landings (landing too fast) will damage your hull
- If your hull reaches 0%, your rocket is destroyed and you'll lose 30% of your money

### Landing
- Approach Earth slowly (speed < 2) for a safe landing
- Earth automatically refuels your rocket when landed
- Only at Earth can you access the shop and sell cargo

## 💎 Resources

### Asteroids
- **Common (Gray)**: Standard resources, found everywhere
- **Rare (Purple)**: Valuable resources worth more money

### Planets
- **Rocky**: Moderate resources, easier to mine
- **Gas**: High-value resources but harder to extract
- **Ice**: Mixed value, medium difficulty

## 🛠️ Upgrades

### Ship Upgrades (Costs increase with each level)

1. **Fuel Tank** - Increase fuel capacity by 50 units
2. **Cargo Bay** - Increase cargo capacity by 50 units
3. **Hull Armor** - Increase hull strength by 25 points
4. **Engine Thrust** - Faster acceleration and higher speed
5. **Mining Laser** - Mine resources faster

## 🔬 Research Technologies

### Fuel Depot Satellite ($5,000)
Deploys 5 orbital fuel stations throughout space. Automatically refuels your rocket when nearby - essential for deep space exploration!

### Resource Scanner ($3,000)
Highlights valuable asteroids (purple) and high-value planets (yellow glow) so you can prioritize the best targets.

### Wormhole Generator ($50,000)
Unlocks wormhole travel to distant galaxies! Each galaxy has increasingly valuable resources:
- **Milky Way** - 1x resource value (starting galaxy)
- **Andromeda** - 2x resource value
- **Triangulum** - 3x resource value
- **Centaurus A** - 5x resource value
- **Whirlpool** - 10x resource value

## 🎮 Gameplay Tips

1. **Start Small**: Make short trips to nearby asteroids, return to Earth, sell cargo, and upgrade
2. **Watch Your Fuel**: Always keep enough fuel to return home! The fuel bar turns yellow when low
3. **Upgrade Strategically**:
   - Fuel tanks let you travel farther
   - Cargo bays let you carry more per trip
   - Engines make navigation easier
   - Mining lasers speed up resource collection
4. **Save for Research**: Fuel depots are game-changing for deep space exploration
5. **Use Fuel Depots**: Once unlocked, plan routes that pass by fuel stations
6. **Scan Before Mining**: The resource scanner helps you avoid wasting time on low-value targets
7. **Master Landing**: Practice soft landings to avoid hull damage and expensive repairs

## 🌌 Progression Path

1. **Early Game** (0-5,000): Mine nearby asteroids, upgrade fuel and cargo
2. **Mid Game** ($5,000-$20,000): Unlock fuel depots, explore deeper space, upgrade all systems
3. **Late Game** ($20,000-$50,000): Max out upgrades, unlock scanner for efficiency
4. **End Game** ($50,000+): Research wormhole travel, explore new galaxies with exponentially better rewards
